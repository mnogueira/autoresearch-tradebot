================================================================================
COMPREHENSIVE TRADING STRATEGY RESEARCH DOCUMENT
Extracted from Academy of Forex YouTube Indicator Testing Playlist (50 Videos)
================================================================================

METHODOLOGY USED ACROSS ALL TESTS:
- Timeframe: Daily chart (1D)
- Test period: 1 year of backtesting per indicator
- Random pair selection (28 forex pairs, randomly chosen)
- Take profit: Based on ATR (Average True Range) - varies by pair
- Stop loss: 1.5x the take profit (e.g., 60 TP / 90 SL, 90 TP / 135 SL, etc.)
- Winning threshold: 60% win rate required to be classified as "winner"
- Position management: Half position closed at 1st TP, remainder moved to breakeven
- TradingView platform used for all testing
- Replay function used for manual backtesting (anti-bias measure)
- Default indicator settings used unless otherwise noted

VIDEO 1: ABSOLUTE STRENGTH HISTOGRAM V2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Indicator Type: Momentum histogram with oscillating lines
What It Measures: Momentum/strength of price movement
Buy/Sell Rules:
  - Oscillating lines determine trend direction (green above red = uptrend)
  - Histogram bars signal entry: green spike = long, red spike = short
  - Both conditions must align: lines + histogram direction
Parameters: Default; added 0.10 threshold line on histogram for filtering
Results: 34 trades, 22 winners = 64% win rate
  - WITH histogram threshold filter (0.10 line): 29 trades, 22 winners = 74% win rate
Winner: YES
Notes:
  - Adding a simple 0.10 threshold line on histogram filtered out losing trades
  - Did not filter out any winning trades in the sample
  - Overtrading indicator but still profitable
  - Suggested combining with Fibonacci levels to identify overextended moves
  - Could work as continuation indicator

VIDEO 4: STOCHASTIC OSCILLATOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Indicator Type: Overbought/Oversold oscillator (K and D lines)
What It Measures: Price momentum relative to high-low range
Buy/Sell Rules:
  - Both K and D lines go into overbought (>80), wait for both to cross back below 80 = SELL
  - Both K and D lines go into oversold (<20), wait for both to cross back above 20 = BUY
  - Signal candle = candle where cross occurs; entry = next candle
Parameters: Default (K=14, D=3, Smooth=3 typical)
Results: 17 trades, 12 winners = ~70% win rate
Winner: YES
Notes:
  - Categorized as entry signal, confirmation, AND volatility indicator
  - Buffer between buy/sell signal lines helps filter choppiness
  - Does not hover in middle enough - swings quickly between OB/OS
  - Space between signal lines reduces whipsaw

KEY STRATEGIC INSIGHTS FROM ALL VIDEOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The 60% win rate with 1:1.5 risk/reward is BREAKEVEN. You need either:
   - Higher win rate, or
   - Better exits to capture more pips per win

2. Indicators that "under-trade" with high accuracy are BETTER than indicators
   that "over-trade" with mediocre accuracy.

3. Every losing trade eliminated is worth more than adding a winning trade
   (especially when it compounds across all pairs).

4. The EMA is the best-performing moving average type across all tests.

5. Moving average color-shift > moving average cross > price break of MA.

6. Multi-line crosses filter out choppiness better than two-line crosses.

7. Indicators with built-in range/chop filters consistently outperform.

8. Stripping complex indicators down to their best component often improves
   performance (Ultimate Trader Oscillator, Cipher B).

9. Confirmation candles (Schaff Trend Cycle method) dramatically improve accuracy.

10. Position management: half off at 1st TP, move remainder to breakeven,
    let ride until exit indicator fires.

11. Weekend gaps can invalidate signals - set orders at Friday's close price
    rather than Monday's open.

12. Large single-candle moves often "rubber band" back - be cautious entering
    after extreme candles.

13. Price action patterns (Head & Shoulders, Cup & Handle, Double Top/Bottom)
    should confirm indicator signals for highest probability trades.

14. The channel tested 263+ indicators total, with ~40% becoming winners (105+).
